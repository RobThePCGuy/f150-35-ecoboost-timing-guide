# Parts

Written for a 2011–2014 F-150 3.5L EcoBoost. Confirm fitment by your VIN before ordering.

## The kits

| Part | Number | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Timing chain kit | Cloyes **9-0738SAVVT3** | 3 chains, crank and idler sprockets, 6 guides, primary + 2 secondary tensioners, 4 phasers, 4 VCT solenoids, 4 phaser bolts, and a new timing chain gear (the chain-driven housing Ford calls the "timing chain gear") with its gasket. No crank bolt. |
| Oil pump | Melling **M390HV** (high volume) or an OE-style pump | Follow the pump maker's sheet. Don't change the relief spring (the video does; see Stage 8). Buy from a known seller; knock-off M390HV pumps have been reported on auction sites (from the video comments). |

## Tools

- 303-1248 camshaft holding plates (2) and a secondary-chain hold-down (303-1530 or equivalent)
- Sockets: 8, 10, 13, 15, 18 mm; deep 10 mm; **TP55 Torx Plus** for phaser bolts; T30 Torx; 17 mm crowfoot / flare-nut wrench
- Inch-pound and foot-pound torque wrenches, angle gauge
- Strap wrench, crank pulley puller and installer, front seal installer, trim tools
- Jack stands, floor jack and strap for the front axle (4WD), drain pans, clean caps and plugs, lint-free wipes
- Zip-top bags, marker, painter's tape, two sheets of cardboard (see [BOLTS.md](BOLTS.md))
- Coolant tester
- Scan tool that runs FORScan (see [RELEARN.md](RELEARN.md))
