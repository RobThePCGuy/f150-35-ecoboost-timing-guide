# Stage 10: Install secondary timing, primary chain and verify marks

This is the heart of the job. The new timing chain gear goes on, then each bank's secondary tensioner, phasers (VCT units) and secondary chain, then the primary chain and its tensioner. You finish by counting chain links between the colored links and photographing it. Nothing gets closed up until that count is right.

Keep the Ford procedure open beside this page for the diagrams and exact wording; this page tells you how we did it and where the traps are.

**Have open:** Ford workshop manual, Section 303-01A: Timing Drive Components – Installation. Use the branch for engines with the secondary tensioners **removed** (new tensioners), Ford installation steps 1–18, then 29 onward. Skip the retained-tensioner branch (steps 19–28).

**Tools / parts / time limits:**
- Camshaft Holding Tool 303-1248, both banks, still installed from Stage 5
- Secondary Chain Hold Down 303-1530 if you need it
- Torx Plus TP55 for the VCT bolts (a plain T55 fits but can cam out)
- Small torque wrench (inch-pounds), a 30+ lb-ft wrench, torque-angle gauge or paint pen
- Soft-jawed vise, small pick, and a lockpin (stiff paper clip or small drill bit) for the primary tensioner
- From the new timing kit: timing chain gear and its gasket, 4 new VCT bolts, 2 new secondary tensioners with blue shipping clips, chains, guides
- Clean engine oil for tensioner O-rings and mega cap bores
- Phone camera for the link-count photo
- Turn the crank clockwise only, ever

## Steps

1. **Timing chain gear.** Put the new gasket from the kit on, set the new gear, and start all 9 bolts. Torque in Ford's sequence to 10 Nm (89 lb-in), then go around the same sequence again adding 45° to each (Ford figure N0124122 in "Timing Drive Components – Installation").
   From bag B07-05: 9 × 8 mm, 10 Nm + 45°, in sequence

2. **RH primary guide.** Install it with its 2 bolts at 10 Nm (89 lb-in) (Ford figure N0128620).
   From bag B07-04

3. **RH secondary tensioner into its bore.** Oil the tensioner's O-rings and the bore in the RH mega cap. Leave the blue shipping clip in; it keeps the tensioner from firing while you push. Drop it in from the top, white shoe end up, square to the bore, and press straight down on the metal body with your thumbs until it snaps and bottoms (Ford figure N0118676). Don't rock it and don't tap it.

4. **RH lower shoe.** The loose white C-shaped piece hooks over the flange on the bottom end of the tensioner. Slide it on so both lips wrap the flange and it sits centered (Ford figure N0118735). It stays on by itself.

5. **Pull the RH shipping clip.** The blue fork sits between the upper white shoe and the body, loop toward the front of the engine. Hook the loop and pull it straight out toward you, then throw it away (Ford figure N0118736). The tensioner stays latched down until you activate it in step 8.

6. **Build the RH secondary set on the bench and hang it.** Lay the RH intake VCT, the RH exhaust VCT and the RH secondary chain together with the colored links on the timing marks (Ford figure N0118117). The intake VCT has one mark. The exhaust VCT has two, an L and an R; use **R** on this bank and **L** on the LH bank. Chain on the intake first, then line the next colored link up with the exhaust mark (from FordTechMakuloco's video). Commenters who skipped this and set the chain on the cam first, then turned the phasers to find the dowels, ended up a tooth off. Hold the whole thing as one unit and offer it to the cam noses, chain over the upper white shoe and under the lower one. Each VCT has a hole on its back that must drop over the dowel pin in the cam. Nudge a cam a few degrees if a hole won't line up, until each VCT sits flush (Ford figure N0118738). Never force it.
   The holding plate has a little play, so a hole can sit just off its dowel. A helper wiggling one VCT while you push the other makes this much easier (common report in the video comments).

7. **RH VCT bolts, new only, four stages.** Holding tool stays on so the cams can't turn (Ford figure N0118739).
   - Stage 1: 40 Nm (30 lb-ft)
   - Stage 2: back each bolt off one full turn
   - Stage 3: 25 Nm (18 lb-ft)
   - Stage 4: +180° (half a turn; paint a line on the head and the VCT face and turn until it points the opposite way)
   Check the colored links are still on their marks. Of the two new bolts, the much longer one goes in the exhaust VCT. Near the end of the 180° the bolt suddenly gets very tight; that's normal. Hold the plate down while you turn, because it can shift (from FordTechMakuloco's video).
   From the new timing kit (bag B07-03 holds the old RH VCT bolts: 2 × TP55, trash them)

8. **Activate the RH secondary tensioner.** This is the one people forget. Put your thumb or a plastic tool handle on the chain right over the upper white shoe, between the two sprockets, and push straight down hard until the shoe bottoms out. Let go fast. It should pop up and pull the chain tight across both shoes (Ford figure N0118740). If the chain is still slack, it didn't fire; push again. Skipping this can wreck the engine.

9. **RH exhaust VCT solenoid.** Keep it clean. Twist it gently into the bore; if it fights you, pull it and look for burrs or dirt on the bore and the solenoid rather than pushing harder, because a cracked mega cap means a cylinder head. Torque to 8 Nm (71 lb-in), then +20° (Ford figure N0118737).
   From bag B07-07

10. **LH secondary tensioner.** Same as the RH: oil the O-rings and the LH mega cap bore, clip left in, white shoe end up, press straight down until it snaps and bottoms (Ford figure N0118741). Hook the white C-shaped lower shoe over the bottom flange, centered (Ford figure N0118742). Pull the blue clip straight out toward the front and throw it away (Ford figure N0118743).

11. **Build and hang the LH secondary set.** Both LH VCT units and the LH secondary chain, colored links on the marks (Ford figure N0118132). Slide them on as one unit, chain over the upper shoe and under the lower one, each VCT's rear hole over its cam dowel, seated flush (Ford figure N0118745).

12. **LH VCT bolts, new only, same four stages.** 40 Nm (30 lb-ft); back off one full turn; 25 Nm (18 lb-ft); +180° (Ford figure N0118746). Re-check the colored links.
    From the new timing kit (bag B07-02 holds the old LH VCT bolts: 2 × TP55, trash them)

13. **Activate the LH secondary tensioner.** Thumb or plastic handle on the chain over the upper white shoe between the LH sprockets, push straight down hard until it bottoms, let go (Ford figure N0118747). The chain should go tight. If it's still slack, push again.

14. **LH exhaust VCT solenoid.** Same care as step 9. 8 Nm (71 lb-in), then +20° (Ford figure N0118744).
    From bag B07-06

15. **Upper LH primary guide.** One bolt, 10 Nm (89 lb-in) (Ford figure N0118125).
    From bag B07-01 (UPPER LH GUIDE)

16. **Check the crank sprocket, don't touch it.** It went on dot facing out in Stage 8 with the oil pump. Look that it's still dot out. Leave it in place.

17. **Primary chain on the marks.** Find the 4 colored links: one for each intake VCT and a pair for the crank. Wrap the chain around the crank sprocket first, with the two colored links on either side of the crank sprocket's mark. Then lay one single colored link on the RH intake VCT mark and one on the LH intake VCT mark (Ford figure N0117970 in "Timing Drive Components – Installation"). If a mark is a tooth off, move the chain a tooth; turn a cam only a hair.
    The video routes it the other way round: it starts at the LH intake VCT, snakes the chain past the phasers while it's still loose, goes around the idler and the RH VCT, and finishes at the crank. Either way the same four marks have to line up; this guide follows Ford's order. A zip tie through the chain and the crank sprocket holds the crank pair on its dot while you fit the guide and tensioner; cut it after the tensioner is fired (common report in the video comments).

18. **Lower LH primary guide.** 2 bolts, 10 Nm (89 lb-in) (Ford figure N0148159).
    From bag B05-04

19. **Primary tensioner arm.** Slide the pivot hole at the top of the long curved arm over its pin and swing the arm in against the outside of the chain (Ford figure N0148158). Nothing bolts on here.

20. **Reset the primary tensioner.** With a small pick, press and hold the ratchet detent (the small round button on the housing) so the toothed ratchet bar can slide. Still holding it, close a soft-jaw vise slowly on the plunger until it's all the way in and the hole in the plunger lines up with the hole in the housing. Push the lockpin through (Ford figure N0143875). If the plunger won't move, the detent isn't released; stop and don't force it.

21. **Bolt the primary tensioner on and fire it.** With the pin still in, set it on its 2 holes, plunger toward the back of the arm. If it won't line up, turn a cam slightly to pull the slack over to the tensioner side. All the slack must be on the tensioner (RH, passenger) run; the LH run from the crank to the LH VCT must be tight. Slack left on the LH side is the usual cause of a one-link-off job that sets a cam correlation code (common report in the video comments). Keep the lockpin in until the bolts are torqued; if it falls out, resetting the plunger in place is a fight. Torque both bolts to 10 Nm (89 lb-in), then pull the lockpin straight out (Ford figure N0148160). The plunger should snap out against the arm and take up the slack.
    From bag B05-03

22. **The link count. Do this before anything else turns the engine.** With all four colored links still on their marks (Ford figure N0122131 in "Timing Drive Components – Installation"):
    - Count **48 links** from the RH intake VCT colored link to the LH intake VCT colored link.
    - Count **35 links** from the LH intake VCT colored link to the pair of colored links at the crank sprocket.
    Count the links *between* the colored links, not including them. Mark every fifth link with a paint pen as you go so you don't lose your place. Photograph both counts and every mark. This exact count is your check; a generic "turn it two revolutions and look" is not a substitute. After the engine turns, the colored links drift away from the marks; that's normal and not a sign of a problem (common report in the video comments). (Ford's Oil Pump procedure has an RH/LH mix-up in the wording of this same check; the Timing Drive Components version above is the one to follow.)

23. **Intake VCT solenoids.** LH intake solenoid and its bolt, then RH intake solenoid and its bolt, each 8 Nm (71 lb-in), then +20° (Ford figures N0118136 for LH, N0118137 for RH). Same care as step 9: clean, slight twist, no force.
    The video torques these solenoid bolts to 89 lb-in (10 Nm) flat. Ford's spec is 8 Nm (71 lb-in) plus 20°; use Ford's.
    From bag B05-05 (LH) and bag B05-06 (RH)

24. **RH cam holding tool off, RH oil tube on.** Remove the RH 303-1248 (Ford figure N0118122). Install the RH valve train oil tube and its 3 bolts at 8 Nm (71 lb-in), then +45° (Ford figure N0118127).
    From bag B05-02

25. **LH cam holding tool off, LH oil tube on.** Remove the LH 303-1248 (Ford figure N0118121). Install the LH valve train oil tube and its 3 bolts at 8 Nm (71 lb-in), then +45° (Ford figure N0118126).
    From bag B05-01

## Done when
- Gear on with new gasket, 9 bolts at 10 Nm + 45° in sequence
- Both secondary tensioners snapped fully into their bores, clips gone, both activated, both secondary chains tight
- All four VCT bolts new and through all four stages
- All four VCT solenoids at 8 Nm + 20°
- Primary tensioner bolted, lockpin pulled, plunger out against the arm
- 48-link and 35-link counts confirmed and photographed
- Both cam holding tools off, both oil tubes on at 8 Nm + 45°

## Stop if
- Any colored link is off its mark, or either count isn't exactly 48 or 35
- A secondary tensioner won't snap fully down, or a secondary chain stays slack after activation
- A VCT won't seat flush over its dowel, or a solenoid needs force to go in
- The primary tensioner plunger won't compress or won't release
- You've lost track of which kit part goes where
