# Stage 7: Remove the phasers and secondary timing parts

Now you strip each cylinder head's front end: the upper LH guide, both pairs of phasers with their secondary chains, the RH guide, the timing chain gear, and both secondary tensioners. This job replaces the secondary tensioners, so they come out and do not go back in.

Photograph and label each bank before you take anything apart.

**Have open:** Ford workshop manual, Section 303-01A: Timing Drive Components – Removal (steps 17–31).

**Tools / parts / time limits:**
- Secondary Chain Hold Down 303-1530
- 303-1248 holding plates still fitted on both banks
- Torx Plus TP55 bit (a plain T55 fits but can cam out), 8 mm socket
- Hand tools only on the guide bolts; no impact or power ratchet
- An "OLD" box for the old phaser bolts, timing chain gear and its gasket, and the old secondary tensioners. The Cloyes kit supplies a new timing chain gear with gasket and four new phaser bolts.

## Steps

1. **Remove the upper LH primary guide** (1 bolt), by hand only. A power tool can crack the guide. Bag it alone. (Ford figure N0118125 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
   Bag B07-01: 1 × 8 mm, back in Stage 10, 10 Nm
2. **Hold the LH secondary tensioner collapsed.** The upper plastic shoe rides on the tensioner plunger under the secondary chain. Press it down toward the tensioner body with your thumb on the chain or a blunt plastic tool. While you hold it, drop the 303-1530 through the hole in the top of the LH mega cap so its tip goes into the hole in the back of the shoe. Let go; the tool now braces against the cap. Don't pry on phaser teeth. (Ford figure N0118130 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
3. **Remove the 2 LH phaser bolts** and drop them in the OLD box. The 303-1248 plate is holding the cams, so don't lean on it as a brace. (Ford figure N0118536 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
   Bag B07-02: 2 × TP55, discard; new bolts from the Cloyes kit go in at Stage 10, 40 Nm, back off one turn, 25 Nm, then +180°
4. **Lift off both LH phasers with the secondary chain** as one unit. Keep them together in the LH tray. (Ford figure N0118537 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
5. **Hold the RH secondary tensioner collapsed** exactly as on the LH side, with the 303-1530 dropped through the RH mega cap into the back of the shoe. (Ford figure N0118538 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
6. **Remove the 2 RH phaser bolts** into the OLD box. (Ford figure N0118540 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
   Bag B07-03: 2 × TP55, discard; new bolts from the Cloyes kit go in at Stage 10, 40 Nm, back off one turn, 25 Nm, then +180°
7. **Lift off both RH phasers with the secondary chain** into the RH tray. (Ford figure N0118539 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
8. **Remove the RH primary guide** (2 bolts), by hand only. (Ford figure N0128620 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
   Bag B07-04: 2 × 8 mm, back in Stage 10, 10 Nm
9. **Remove the timing chain gear** (9 bolts). That's the aluminum housing with its own sprocket and inlet pipe. Put it and its red gasket straight into the OLD box; the kit has a new one. Push the 9 bolts through a cardboard outline of the housing in case lengths differ. (Ford figure N0124121 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
   Bag B07-05: 9 × 8 mm, back in Stage 10, 10 Nm + 45° in Ford's sequence
10. **Pull the LH exhaust oil control solenoid** (1 bolt). Twist slightly as you pull, then cap or bag it clean. (Ford figure N0118541 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
    Bag B07-06: 1 × 8 mm, back in Stage 10, 8 Nm + 20°
11. **Remove the LH lower tensioner shoe.** It's a plastic U-channel slipped onto the bottom of the tensioner body. Grip it and slide it off. Don't lever against the cam journal faces. (Ford figure N0118542 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
12. **Remove the LH secondary tensioner.** It isn't bolted. Push it up from its bottom end so it slides up its slot in the mega cap, then lift it out toward you. Rock it gently if tight; never pry on the cap's machined faces. OLD box. (Ford figure N0118543 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
13. **Pull the RH exhaust oil control solenoid** (1 bolt) the same way and keep it clean. (Ford figure N0118544 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
    Bag B07-07: 1 × 8 mm, back in Stage 10, 8 Nm + 20°
14. **Remove the RH lower tensioner shoe** by sliding it off the base of the body. (Ford figure N0118611 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
15. **Remove the RH secondary tensioner**: push up from the bottom, lift out, rock gently if needed. OLD box. (Ford figure N0118613 in "Timing Drive Components – Removal")
16. **Match old to new.** Lay each removed part beside its replacement from the kit and confirm they're the same shape and fit before you start rebuilding.

## Done when
- Both phaser pairs and secondary chains off, kept by bank
- Upper LH and RH primary guides off
- Timing chain gear and gasket off and in the OLD box
- Both secondary tensioners and shoes off; old tensioners in the OLD box, not to be reused
- All four exhaust and intake solenoids out, clean and capped
- Old phaser bolts discarded; four new bolts from the kit set aside
- Bags B07-01 through B07-07 filled and labeled

## Stop if
- A cam turns once the phasers come off, or a 303-1248 plate shifts
- A new part doesn't match the old one
- A mega cap bore or tensioner slot is damaged
- You find wear or contamination you can't explain
