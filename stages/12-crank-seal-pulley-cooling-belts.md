# Stage 12: Install crank seal, pulley, cooling parts and belts

With the front cover sealed, the front of the engine gets its parts back: a new crank seal, the crank pulley on a new bolt, the belt tensioner and generator, the new coolant pump and thermostat housing, the A/C stretch belt and the main accessory belt. Coolant and oil both wait.

Keep the Ford procedure open beside this page for the diagrams and exact wording; this page tells you how we did it and where the traps are.

**Have open:** Ford workshop manual, Section 303-01A: Crankshaft Front Seal – Installation; Crankshaft Pulley – Installation; Engine Front Cover – Installation (steps 11–14). Section 303-03: Coolant Pump — 3.5L GTDI, 3.7L (Installation); Thermostat Housing — 3.5L GTDI (Installation). Section 303-05: Air Conditioning (A/C) Compressor Belt — 3.5L GTDI, 3.7L, 5.0L (Installation, the early-build branch that covers the 3.5L).

**Tools / parts / time limits:**
- Front Crankshaft Seal Installer 303-1251 with Crankshaft Vibration Damper Installer 303-102
- Front Cover Oil Seal Installer 303-335 with 303-102 for the pulley
- Strap Wrench 303-D055 or equivalent
- Torque wrenches to 120 Nm, torque-angle gauge or paint pen, breaker bar for turning the crank
- Hose-clamp pliers, a tie strap at least 3 mm (0.12 in) wide, side cutters, 15 mm socket for the belt tensioner
- New crank front seal, new crank pulley bolt (reuse the washer), new coolant pump with O-ring and gasket, new thermostat housing gasket and O-ring
- Clean engine oil and clean coolant
- Keep oil off the front-cover RTV joints; your earliest oil-contact time from Stage 11 still stands
- Turn the crank clockwise only

## Steps

1. **New crank front seal.** Oil the seal bore in the front cover, staying off the RTV joints. Press the new seal in square with 303-1251 and 303-102 (Ford figure N0114361 in "Crankshaft Front Seal – Installation").

2. **Oil the seal lip and pulley.** Wipe clean oil on the inner lip of the new seal and on the pulley's outer sealing surface. Again, none on the RTV.

3. **Press the pulley on.** Use 303-335 and 303-102 to draw the pulley onto the crank nose (Ford figure N0120136 in "Crankshaft Pulley – Installation").

4. **New pulley bolt, four stages.** Wrap the 303-D055 strap around the pulley rim so it stops the pulley turning clockwise. Have a helper hold the handle or brace it against a solid frame point, fingers out of the pinch. Put on the original washer, same face toward the pulley as it came off, then the new bolt (Ford figure N0120135):
   - Stage 1: 120 Nm (89 lb-ft)
   - Stage 2: back it off one full turn
   - Stage 3: 50 Nm (37 lb-ft)
   - Stage 4: +90° (1/4 turn; paint a line)
   Bag B04-09: 1 × 18 mm, the old bolt, trash it; the new bolt goes in. From bag B04-10 (CRANK WASHER — REUSE)

5. **Accessory belt tensioner.** 2 bolts, 25 Nm (18 lb-ft).
   From bag B04-08

6. **Generator stud and generator.** Thread the stud back in, the same end into the engine as it came out, to 8 Nm (71 lb-in). Swing the generator back into place and torque its bolt to 48 Nm (35 lb-ft).
   From bag B04-07 (stud) and bag B04-06 (generator bolt)

7. **Coolant pump.** Fit the new O-ring in the pump and wet it with clean coolant. Put the new gasket on, press the pump over the channel cover plate snout sticking out of the front cover, and start all 7 bolts by hand. Torque in Ford's sequence to 10 Nm (89 lb-in), then around the sequence again +45° each (Ford figure N0129613 in "Coolant Pump – Installation").
   From bag B04-05: 7 × 8 mm, 10 Nm + 45°, in sequence

8. **Coolant pump pulley, finger tight for now.** Set the pulley on and start its 4 bolts by hand. They get torqued at the end, after the belt is on.
   From bag B04-04

9. **Thermostat housing.** New gasket and new O-ring, O-ring wetted with clean coolant. Set the housing on, start the 4 bolts, and torque in Ford's sequence to 8 Nm (71 lb-in), then +45° each (Ford figure N0124486 in "Thermostat Housing – Installation").
   From bag B04-03: 4 × 8 mm, 8 Nm + 45°, in sequence

10. **Hoses onto the housing.** For each one, push the hose on until it bottoms against the bead, squeeze the spring clamp with hose-clamp pliers, slide it over the bead and let go just behind the hose end with the tabs where you can reach them. Twist the hose a little to make sure it's seated. In order: upper radiator hose, heater outlet hose, lower radiator hose, then the small degas bottle hose on the top nipple.

11. **LH turbo-to-CAC tube bracket.** 1 bolt, 6 Nm (53 lb-in) (Ford figure N0132555 in "Thermostat Housing – Installation"). Plug in the block heater connector if your truck has one. The thermostat appearance cover waits for Stage 14.
    From bag B04-02

12. **Take the plastic off the belts.** Pull off the waterproof plastic you wrapped over the belts in Stage 4 to keep coolant off them, plus any packaging on the new A/C stretch belt. Nothing plastic stays near a pulley.

13. **Feed the A/C stretch belt.** From under the truck, push the belt up between the crank pulley and the accessory belt tensioner, let it hang behind the crank pulley with the ribs facing forward, then loop the bottom onto the A/C compressor pulley, seated in its grooves and riding **above the oil pan flange** (Ford figure N0117717 in "A/C Compressor Belt – Installation"; Ford's art shows a 5.0L, the 3.5L is the same idea).

14. **Tie-strap it to the crank pulley.** Lay the belt over the top of the crank pulley. Feed a tie strap at least 3 mm wide through the pulley spokes, up and over the belt, and pull it tight (Ford figure N0117723).

15. **Roll it on, clockwise.** Breaker bar on the crank bolt, turn clockwise as you face the engine. The strap drags the belt onto the pulley. Watch the lower run the whole time: it must stay above the oil pan flange or it gets chewed. Stop when the strap reaches 6 o'clock (Ford figure N0117759).

16. **Cut the strap and seat-check.** Snip the strap and pull it out of the spokes. Turn the crank clockwise one more full turn (Ford figure N0117760). Sight along both pulleys: every rib in its groove, nothing hanging off an edge. If a rib is off, redo the tie-strap roll-on. This is a separate clockwise turn, done only now that the Stage 10 link count is photographed.

17. **Main accessory belt.** Put a 15 mm socket on the tensioner, swing it counterclockwise to slack it, and route the belt to match the photo you took in Stage 4 and Ford's 3.5L routing in "Front End Accessory Drive (FEAD) – Exploded View" (Section 303-05). Let the tensioner back down slowly and check the belt sits square in every pulley.

18. **Torque the coolant pump pulley.** Now that the belt holds it, the 4 pulley bolts go to 25 Nm (18 lb-ft). Write it down.
    From bag B04-04

19. **Leave fluids out.** No coolant fill or bleed, no oil fill yet. Those wait for Stage 15.

## Done when
- New crank seal in square, pulley on, new bolt through all four stages
- Tensioner at 25 Nm, generator stud at 8 Nm, generator bolt at 48 Nm
- Coolant pump at 10 Nm + 45° and thermostat housing at 8 Nm + 45°, both in sequence, all four hoses clamped
- A/C belt above the pan flange, tie strap removed, one clockwise seat-check turn done, every rib in its groove
- Accessory belt routed to match your photo
- Coolant pump pulley bolts at 25 Nm and recorded

## Stop if
- The crank fights you or gets suddenly hard to turn while you roll the belt on
- The A/C belt drops below the oil pan flange or gets pinched
- A hose won't seat fully, or a coolant O-ring is cut or rolled
- The belt routing doesn't match your photo and Ford's 3.5L routing
