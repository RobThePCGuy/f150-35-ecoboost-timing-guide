# Stage 11: Prepare and seal the front cover

The timing is in and counted, so the front cover goes on. You clean the cover and the engine's sealing faces two different ways, lay RTV, and run two bolt groups in three stages each, with two odd bolts (21 and 22) that get their own torque. Two clocks run from the first squeeze of RTV, and a third one decides when oil can touch it.

Keep the Ford procedure open beside this page for the diagrams and exact wording; this page tells you how we did it and where the traps are.

**Have open:** Ford workshop manual, Section 303-01A: Engine Front Cover – Installation.

**Tools / parts / time limits:**
- 3M Roloc Bristle Disc, 2 in white (part 07528), on a tool that turns about 15,000 rpm, for the removed cover only
- Silicone gasket remover, Motorcraft metal surface prep (ZC-31-B), plastic scraper, lint-free towels
- Motorcraft TA-357 High Performance Engine RTV (in date)
- Spindle Bearing Installer 205-149 with Handle 205-153, only if the radial seal is coming out
- Small torque wrench (inch-pounds), torque-angle gauge or paint pen, a timer
- Your cardboard bolt map from Stage 4, all 22 holes numbered
- **10 minutes** from the first squeeze of RTV: cover on, bolts 1, 2, 7, 8, 15, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21 in and through their stages
- **35 minutes** from the first squeeze of RTV: every remaining cover bolt and the engine mount bracket bolts in and tight
- **90 minutes** minimum after the cover is on before the RTV can see engine oil

## Steps

1. **Confirm Stage 10 is signed.** Link counts photographed, both cam tools off, both oil tubes on. Once the cover is on you can't see the marks again.

2. **Clean the removed cover.** On the bench only, take the old RTV off the cover with the white bristle disc at about 15,000 rpm. No metal scrapers, wire wheels or other abrasive discs. Then wash the whole cover thoroughly so no grit from the disc is left anywhere on it, and dry it.

3. **Clean the engine faces by hand.** On the block, both heads and the front of the oil pan, no bristle disc, no wire brush, no power abrasive of any kind. Stuff clean lint-free towels into the openings first. Scrape off the big chunks with plastic, put silicone gasket remover on and let it sit a few minutes, wipe it off, and do a second round if any RTV is left. Get the old RTV that squeezed onto the inside faces of the block and heads too. Finish with metal surface prep to take off the last oil film. Staining is normal; don't chase shiny metal. Lift the towels out slowly so nothing drops in.

4. **Check the two locating dowels.** Both must be fully seated in the block.

5. **Radial seal, only if it came out.** If you removed the cover's radial seal, press a new one in from the back side of the cover with 205-149 (Ford figure N0174428 in "Engine Front Cover – Installation"). If it never came out, leave it.

6. **Rehearse.** Hang the cover on the dowels dry once, start a couple of first-group bolts by hand, and take it back off. Lay the first-group bolts (1, 2, 7, 8, 15, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21) where your hand finds them in order. Have the torque wrench set to 10 Nm.

7. **Lay the RTV.** Start the timer with the first squeeze. Run a 3.0 mm (0.11 in) bead along the inside edge of the cover's sealing surface, including around the 2 bolt bosses Ford marks. Then put a heavier 5.5 mm (0.21 in) bead at the 5 joint spots Ford marks: where the oil pan meets the block and where each head meets the block (Ford figure N0130510 in "Engine Front Cover – Installation").

8. **Hang the cover and run the first group, three stages, inside 10 minutes.** Slide it straight onto the dowels. Start bolts 1, 2, 7, 8, 15, 17, 18, 19, 20 and 21 (Ford figure N0130115 in "Engine Front Cover – Installation").
   - **Stage 1:** bolts 1, 2, 7, 8, 15, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21 in Ford's sequence to 10 Nm (89 lb-in)
   - **Stage 2:** bolts 1, 2, 7, 8, 15, 17, 18, 19, 20 (not 21) in sequence to 20 Nm (177 lb-in), then each +45°
   - **Stage 3:** bolt 21 alone to 20 Nm (177 lb-in), then **+90°**
   Every bolt goes back in the hole it came from on your cardboard template (the 15 mm and the small bolt only fit their own holes).
   From bag B04-13: 9 × 13 mm, 10 Nm, then 20 Nm + 45° in sequence
   From bag B04-14: bolt 21, the larger center bolt above the crank, 10 Nm, then 20 Nm + 90°
   Write down the time the cover went on.

9. **Run the second group, three stages, inside 35 minutes.** Start the remaining 12 bolts: 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16 and 22 (Ford figure N0130119 in "Engine Front Cover – Installation").
   - **Stage 1:** bolts 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16 in sequence to 10 Nm (89 lb-in)
   - **Stage 2:** the same 11 bolts in sequence to 20 Nm (177 lb-in), then each +45°
   - **Stage 3:** bolt 22 alone to **10 Nm (89 lb-in), then +45°**. Bolt 22 never sees 20 Nm.
   The engine mount bracket bolts also have to be in and tight inside this same 35 minutes.
   From bag B04-15: 11 × 13 mm, 10 Nm, then 20 Nm + 45°
   From bag B04-16: bolt 22, the small 8 or 10 mm bolt, 10 Nm + 45°

10. **Generator harness.** Slip the generator harness bracket over the front cover stud bolt, then push the black harness clip into its hole on the lower edge of the cover until it clicks flat (Ford figure N0121562). Keep the harness clear of where the crank pulley will spin.

11. **Cooler tube bracket.** Set the transmission cooler tube bracket over the same stud bolt and run the nut down to 12 Nm (106 lb-in) (Ford figure N0121561).
    From bag B04-12

12. **Pan push-pin.** Line the harness clip's hole up with the hole in the oil pan boss and push the pin in. If it has a center plug, pull the plug up, push the body in flush, then press the plug down to lock it. Use a new pin if the legs are broken (Ford figure N0121573).
    From bag B04-11

13. **Write down the oil deadline.** Cover installed at: ________. Earliest time the RTV may touch engine oil, which is also the earliest you may fill oil (installed time plus at least 90 minutes): ________. The actual fill time gets written down in Stage 15. Tight bolts don't mean you can fill. Stage 12 puts a little oil on the crank seal bore, seal lip and pulley; keep that oil off every RTV joint.

## Done when
- Both cleaning methods used in the right places: bristle disc on the cover only, chemical and hand cleaning on the engine
- First group through all three stages inside 10 minutes, bolt 21 at 20 Nm + 90°
- Second group through all three stages inside 35 minutes, bolt 22 at 10 Nm + 45°
- Engine mount bracket bolts tight inside 35 minutes
- Harness, cooler bracket nut and pan push-pin back on
- Cover install time and earliest oil-contact time written down

## Stop if
- The 10-minute or 35-minute window runs out: take the cover off, remove all the RTV, clean with gasket remover and surface prep, and do it again
- A dowel isn't seated, or the cover won't sit flat on both dowels
- You find a scratch or gouge across a sealing face
- A bolt doesn't match its hole on your map, or you've mixed up 21 and 22
- RTV got smeared, contaminated, or touched by oil
