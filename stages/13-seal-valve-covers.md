# Stage 13: Seal both valve covers and restore their connections

Both valve covers go back on with new gaskets and fresh RTV at the front-cover joints, and everything that lives on top of them gets hooked back up: VCT solenoid plugs, harness clips, the RH cam sensors, the PCM plug, the wastegate hoses and the CAC tube brackets. Coils, the fuel pump, the intake and the air pipes come back in Stage 14. Keep the Ford procedures open alongside this page for the diagrams and exact wording.

**Video:**
- [Part 2 at 31:00](https://youtu.be/k4bUkK9eqKk?t=1860) — cleaning the cover grooves, greasing the tube seals and seating the new gaskets
- [Part 4 at 8:00](https://youtu.be/dzb0PMI5v78?t=480) — RTV at the four front-cover T-joints and setting the covers

**Have open:** Ford workshop manual, Section 303-01A: Valve Cover – LH (Installation); Valve Cover – RH (Installation).

**Tools / parts / time limits:**
- Motorcraft TA-357 High Performance Engine RTV (check the date on the tube), silicone gasket remover and metal surface prep for cleanup
- New LH and RH valve cover gaskets; new VCT solenoid and spark plug tube seals only if you pulled damaged ones (seal installer tools for those)
- 10 mm deep-well socket, 8 mm, 10 mm and 15 mm sockets, inch-pound torque wrench, timer
- New zip ties, including the anchor-foot kind; clean engine oil for the CMP sensor O-rings
- **4 minutes** from laying the RTV to having that cover's bolts torqued. Do one cover at a time.

## Steps

### LH cover

1. **Seals, only if needed.** If you removed damaged spark plug tube seals or VCT solenoid seals from the LH cover, press new ones in now with the seal installers. Undamaged seals stay put.
   A thin film of grease on the spark plug tube seals lets the cover slide down the tubes without rolling them (from FordTechMakuloco's video). Check every tube seal is fully seated afterward; a seal that isn't seated lets oil into the plug well.
2. **Clean and dry-fit.** Clean the old oil out of the groove with air and a swab dampened with cleaner. Find the straight section of the new gasket with a small mark at each end; that's the gasket's end. Start at the front corners, press the gasket in at the molded curves first, then work the straight runs in, and go around once more to make sure it's fully seated so it can't fall out (from FordTechMakuloco's video). Put the new gasket in the cover's groove. Make sure the front-cover-to-head joints are clean and oil-free. Set your timer and have all 11 bolts within reach before you open the RTV.
3. **RTV at the joints.** Lay an 8 mm (0.31 in) bead of TA-357 on the two spots where the front cover meets the LH cylinder head (Ford figure N0120133 in "Valve Cover – LH, Installation"). Start the timer.
4. **Set the cover and torque it.** Lower the cover straight down so the gasket doesn't roll, start all 11 bolt-and-sleeve assemblies by hand, then tighten to **10 Nm (89 lb-in)** in Ford's sequence (Ford figure N0124241 in "Valve Cover – LH, Installation"). If you blow past 4 minutes, pull the cover, scrape off the RTV, clean with gasket remover and surface prep, and start over. `From bag B03-18`
5. **Check the VCT seals.** Look down each of the 2 round VCT holes. The rubber seal should sit around the solenoid's plastic connector collar with its top edge below the top of the collar (Ford figure N0124002). If one has rolled up onto the connector, press it down evenly all the way around with your thumb. No screwdrivers; they tear the lip. A seal riding high leaks oil.
6. **Harness clips.** Each clip pushes into a hole in the cover or down over a stud. Line it up square, press it in with your thumb until it snaps flat, then tug. A clip that spins or pulls out has broken barbs; replace it.
   These clips aren't sold on their own. A common fix is to drill a small hole in the clip base and hold the harness with a zip tie (common report in the video comments).
7. **VCT plugs.** Connect the 2 LH VCT solenoid plugs, each to the solenoid its wire runs to, push until it clicks and tug to confirm. Add a new zip tie where the old one was cut (Ford figure N0146628 in "Valve Cover – LH, Installation").
8. **CAC tube bracket.** Fit the bracket and run both nuts on by hand. Torque the small M6 nut to **6 Nm (53 lb-in)** and the large M10 nut to **48 Nm (35 lb-ft)**. Use the inch-pound wrench on the small one; it strips easily (Ford figure N0128827). `From bags B03-16 and B03-17`
9. **Wastegate hose.** Push the small rubber hose straight over the nipple on the round wastegate actuator until it bottoms; a slight twist helps (Ford figure N0146624). There's no clamp. Tug it. A loose hose here sets a boost fault.
10. **Wastegate hose tie.** Push a new anchor-foot zip tie into its hole in the cover until it locks, loop it around the hose where the old one sat, snug it without flattening the hose and trim the tail (Ford figure N0146623).
11. **Dipstick.** Slide the oil level indicator back in and seat it fully. `From bag B03-15`

### RH cover

12. **Seals, only if needed.** Same as the LH side: new tube or VCT seals only where you removed damaged ones.
13. **RTV at the joints.** New gasket in the groove, joints clean, timer ready, 12 stud bolts in reach. Lay an 8 mm (0.31 in) bead of TA-357 on the two front-cover-to-RH-head joints (Ford figure N0120132 in "Valve Cover – RH, Installation"). Start the timer.
14. **Set the cover and torque it.** Lower it straight down, start all 12 stud bolts by hand, then tighten to **10 Nm (89 lb-in)** in Ford's sequence (Ford figure N0147483 in "Valve Cover – RH, Installation"). Same 4-minute rule; miss it and you clean and redo. `From bag B03-21`
15. **Check the VCT seals.** Same check as step 5 (Ford figure N0124002). Seal top edge below the connector collar, pressed down by thumb if it rolled.
16. **Harness clips.** Snap every harness retainer back onto the cover holes and stud bolts, square and flat, then tug. Replace any with broken barbs.
17. **Cam sensors.** Wipe a film of clean engine oil on both CMP sensor O-rings, push the 2 sensors into their bores, and torque the 2 bolts to **7 Nm (62 lb-in)** (Ford figure N0146631 in "Valve Cover – RH, Installation"). Plug in both sensor connectors until they click (Ford figure N0146626). `From bag B03-20`
18. **VCT plugs and tie.** Connect the 2 RH VCT plugs, each to its own solenoid. Line the latch up with the lock on the collar and push straight down until it clicks; if it won't go, check the latch side instead of forcing it. Put new zip ties around the wastegate hose and engine wiring at the lower edge of the cover (Ford figure N0146629).
19. **PCM plug and inline connector.** Swing the PCM plug's lever open, set the plug square on the header, then swing the lever closed so it draws the plug down and latches. Push only on the lever. Push the lower inline connector together until it clicks and snap its 2 wire retainers onto their mounts (Ford figure N0129122). Tug everything.
20. **Retainer and tie on top.** Push the wire retainer's anchor into its hole on top of the cover until it sits flat, then add a new zip tie around the harness at the lower point shown (Ford figure N0147480).
21. **Wastegate hose.** Push the hose fully onto the actuator nipple (no clamp), tug it, then anchor a new zip tie in the cover and snug it around the hose without crushing it (Ford figure N0147481).
22. **Coolant hose and CAC bracket.** Press the coolant hose clip back over its valve cover stud. Set the CAC tube bracket over both studs so it traps the hose (Ford figure N0128663). Run the 2 nuts on by hand and torque to **6 Nm (53 lb-in)** with the inch-pound wrench. `From bag B03-19`

## Done when
- Both covers torqued to 10 Nm in sequence, each inside its 4-minute window, with new gaskets
- All 4 VCT seals sit below their connector collars
- RH CMP sensors in at 7 Nm, O-rings oiled, plugs clicked
- Every VCT plug, the PCM plug, the inline connector and every harness clip latched and tug-tested
- Both wastegate hoses on and tied; both CAC brackets torqued; RH coolant hose trapped under its bracket
- Dipstick seated
- Coils, fuel pump, intake and air pipes still off for Stage 14

## Stop if
- You missed a 4-minute window: pull that cover, clean all RTV off and redo it
- A VCT seal rides up on its connector and won't press down evenly
- A connector is cracked, a terminal is bent, or a plug won't latch
- You aren't sure how a hose or harness is routed. Check your removal photos and the Ford figures before going on.
