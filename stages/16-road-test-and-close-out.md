# Stage 16: Road test, cold-soak check and close-out

The truck runs and the relearn is done. Now prove the repair fixed the complaint. The original rattle was a cold-start fault, so a warm idle proves nothing. The real test is a cold start after an overnight sit. This stage drives it gently, lets it sit, starts it cold, rechecks everything and closes out your work record. Keep the Ford procedures open alongside this page for reference.

**Have open:** Your work log and torque log; the owner's manual oil and coolant check pages; [RELEARN.md](../RELEARN.md) if MP_LRN needs another look.

**Tools / parts / time limits:**
- Scan tool (FORScan Lite + OBDLink MX+ works) for codes and data logging
- Flashlight, rags, coolant and 5W-30 for top-ups
- At least one overnight cold soak before you call it done

## Steps

1. **Easy road test.** Drive it gently first: normal throttle, no towing, no hard boost. Watch the gauges and listen. Note mileage, route and conditions.
2. **Look underneath after.** Park, let it sit a few minutes, then check under the front cover, oil pan, valve cover edges, fuel pump area, coolant pump and hoses for drips or seepage.
3. **Scan.** Pull codes and save the scan. Confirm MP_LRN still reads YES and there's no P0315 or cam/crank correlation code.
   If a cam/crank correlation code (P0016 to P0019) sets right after this job, the usual cause reported in the comments is a chain a tooth off, often from slack left on the LH side of the primary chain or the crank moving while the chain was off. Re-check the timing before replacing sensors or solenoids (common report in the video comments).
4. **Cold soak.** Let it sit overnight, or as long as it sat when the rattle used to show up.
5. **Cold start.** Start it cold and listen hard for the first few seconds, the way you heard the original rattle. Record the result, ambient temperature and how long it sat.
6. **Recheck levels cold.** Oil by the engine-off method, coolant at the degas bottle marks. Top up as needed and record what you added.
7. **Repeat for a few heat cycles.** Recheck oil, coolant and leaks after the next few drives and cold starts. Coolant can drop a little as the last air works out.
8. **Close the record.** Write down final mileage, cold-start results, scan files, fluids and parts used, and anything you saw that needs follow-up later.

## Done when
- Road test finished with no warnings, noise or drivability problems
- Cold start after an overnight sit is quiet, and recorded
- No codes; MP_LRN = YES
- Oil and coolant levels stable through several heat cycles; no leaks
- Work log closed with mileage, conditions, results and any follow-up items

## Stop if
- The rattle or any new noise comes back on a cold start
- The oil pressure warning comes on, or any timing, cam or crank code sets
- Any leak shows up: oil, coolant or fuel
- It runs rough, hesitates or loses power

Any of these means diagnose before you keep driving it.
