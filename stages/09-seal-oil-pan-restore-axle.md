# Stage 9: Seal the oil pan; restore axle and starter

The oil pump and crank sprocket are on, so the pan goes back now, before any chains or the front cover. You lay RTV, set the pan, align it, and run three torque stages against a clock. Then the axle, crossmember, starter, filter, cooler tubes and underbody pieces go back. No oil goes in yet.

Keep the Ford procedure open beside this page for the diagrams and exact wording; this page tells you how we did it and where the traps are.

**Have open:** Ford workshop manual, Section 303-01A: Oil Pan (Installation). Section 303-06: Starter Motor (Removal and Installation). Section 501-02: Front End Body Panels – Exploded View (air dam).

**Tools / parts / time limits:**
- Motorcraft TA-357 High Performance Engine RTV (in date), metal surface prep (ZC-31-B), lint-free towels
- 3/8 torque wrench that reads inch-pounds, a larger one for 48–115 Nm, torque-angle gauge or paint pen
- Timer you can see from under the truck
- Jack and strap to raise the front axle
- New oil filter
- **4 minutes** from the first squeeze of RTV: pan on, bolts 10, 11, 13, 14 in, pan aligned
- **60 minutes** from the first squeeze of RTV: all three final pan stages done
- Order is fixed: pan Stage 1, then Stage 2, then Stage 3, then the bellhousing bolts

## Steps

1. **Dry run first.** The pan was cleaned in Stage 6. Wipe both flanges once more with surface prep. Push all 16 pan bolts through a cardboard outline numbered like Ford's pan diagram (Ford figure N0114565 in "Oil Pan – Installation"). Lift the pan into place with no RTV, slide it, and find bolt holes 10, 11, 13 and 14 so you know where your hands go. Start the timer only once you can do this without hunting. Skip the front-cover-to-block bead Ford shows in its first step: the front cover is off, so there is nothing to put it on.

2. **Lay the perimeter bead.** Cut the nozzle for a 3 mm (0.11 in) bead. Run it along the inside edge of the pan's sealing flange, all the way around (Ford figure N0130513 in "Oil Pan – Installation"). Start the timer the moment the RTV starts coming out.

3. **Add the two heavy dabs at the rear corners.** Re-cut the nozzle to give just under 1/4 in (5.5 mm). At the two rear corners of the pan flange, where the rear seal retainer plate meets the block, lay a short thick bar straight across the flange on top of the 3 mm bead (Ford figure N0117186 in "Oil Pan – Installation").

4. **Set the pan, slide it forward.** Hold the pan tight against the transmission as you lift it, then slide it forward toward the front of the engine so the bead doesn't wipe on the block. Start bolts 10, 11, 13 and 14 finger tight (Ford figure N0114563 in "Oil Pan – Installation"). This all has to happen inside 4 minutes.
   From bag B06-16 (bolt 10) and bag B06-17 (bolts 11, 13, 14)

5. **Start the bellhousing bolts loose.** Thread in the 2 LH bellhousing-to-pan bolts (Ford figure N0113916) and the 1 RH pan-to-bellhousing bolt below the starter (Ford figure N0113914). Leave them loose for now.
   From bag B06-15 (2 LH) and bag B06-14 (1 RH)

6. **Align the pan side to side.** Push the pan sideways until it sits flush with the block at the two points Ford marks (Ford figure N0114564 in "Oil Pan – Installation"). Feel it with a fingertip or a straightedge. Flush, not proud, at both.

7. **Pull the four locating bolts down light.** Bolts 10, 11, 13, 14, in Ford's order, to 3 Nm (27 lb-in). That holds the alignment while you work.

8. **Run in the rest and do the three final stages.** Start the remaining bolts. Then, in this order, inside 60 minutes of the first RTV:
   - **Stage 1:** bolts 1–9 and 11–14 (skip 10) to 20 Nm (177 lb-in) in number order, then each one +45°.
   - **Stage 2:** bolts 15 and 16 to 10 Nm (89 lb-in), then +45°.
   - **Stage 3:** bolt 10 last, to 20 Nm (177 lb-in), then +90°.
   45° is 1/8 turn and 90° is 1/4 turn. Use the angle gauge or paint a line on each head, and make each angle one smooth pull.
   From bag B06-18 (bolts 1–9 and 12, 20 Nm +45°), bag B06-19 (bolts 15 and 16, 8 mm, 10 Nm +45°)

9. **Now torque the bellhousing bolts.** Only after pan Stage 3: the 2 LH bellhousing-to-pan bolts to 48 Nm (35 lb-ft), then the RH pan-to-bellhousing bolt to 48 Nm (35 lb-ft). Write down the time Stage 3 finished.
   From bag B06-15 and bag B06-14

10. **Clip the generator harness back to the block.** Line the black push-in retainer up with its hole beside the pan rail and push it in until the head sits flat (Ford figure N0121583 in "Oil Pan – Installation"). If the barbs are chewed, use a new one or a zip tie. Keep the harness off the exhaust and the belt.
    From bag B06-20

11. **Raise the axle and bolt it up.** Jack the front axle carrier up slowly and watch the vacuum lines to the axle disconnect solenoid as it rises; they pull off or kink easily. Install and torque each bushing bolt to 115 Nm (85 lb-ft): lower carrier bolt, axle tube bolt, then upper carrier bolt. Then set the crossmember and torque its 4 nuts and bolts to 90 Nm (66 lb-ft) (Ford figure N0085027 in "Oil Pan – Installation").
    From bag B06-13 (LOWER), bag B06-12 (AXLE TUBE), bag B06-11 (upper), bag B06-10 (crossmember, 8 pieces)

12. **Reinstall the starter.** The battery stays disconnected. Put the starter up, start both mounting bolts, and torque the **upper first**, then the lower, both to 48 Nm (35 lb-ft). The ground cable eyelet rides on the lower one. Then the cooler tube bracket nut on the starter to 12 Nm (106 lb-in). Put the thin S-wire eyelet on the small stud and torque its nut to 5 Nm (44 lb-in) by hand with an inch-pound wrench; it strips easily. Put the heavy battery cable on the big B+ stud and torque to 12 Nm (106 lb-in), tug the cable to make sure it won't spin, and turn the S eyelet so it can't touch the B+ stud. Snap the terminal cover back on.
    From bag B06-09 (mount bolts, LOWER + GROUND), bag B06-08 (cooler bracket nut), bag B06-07 (S nut), bag B06-06 (B+ nut), bag B06-05 (terminal cover)

13. **New oil filter.** Wipe the filter seat clean. Leave the new filter's gasket dry; don't oil it. Spin it on until the gasket touches, snug it to 5 Nm (44 lb-in), mark the can at 12 o'clock and turn it another 180° until the mark is at 6 o'clock.

14. **Cooler tubes.** Set the transmission cooler tubes in place, snap the 2 retainers, and torque the bracket bolt to 12 Nm (106 lb-in).
    From bag B06-04

15. **Underbody.** Put back only what you took off: the front air dam (per the Section 501-02 exploded view), the skid plate with its 4 bolts at 48 Nm (35 lb-ft), and the splash shield with its 4 fasteners.
    From bag B06-03 (air dam), bag B06-02 (skid plate), bag B06-01 (splash shield)

16. **Leave the oil out.** Engine oil and the battery ground both wait for Stage 15.

## Done when
- Pan sits flush with the block at both alignment points
- Pan Stages 1, 2 and 3 done in order inside 60 minutes, and the finish time written down
- Bellhousing bolts at 48 Nm, torqued after Stage 3
- Axle bushing bolts at 115 Nm, crossmember at 90 Nm, axle vacuum lines connected and undamaged
- Starter mounted, S and B+ nuts torqued, cover on, battery still disconnected
- New filter on dry, cooler tubes and underbody pieces back
- No oil in the engine

## Stop if
- The 4-minute or 60-minute window runs out: pull the pan, strip all RTV, clean with remover and surface prep, and start over
- The pan won't sit flush at both points, or RTV got wiped or contaminated while setting it
- You can't tell which bolt is which number
- The axle won't hold steady on the jack, or a vacuum line came off or kinked
