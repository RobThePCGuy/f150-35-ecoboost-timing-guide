# Stage 3: Intake, fuel pump, coils and valve covers

Clear the top of the engine: the intake pipes, the intake manifold, the high-pressure fuel pump, all six coils, and both valve covers. Several Ford procedures in this stage repeat each other (the appearance cover and air pipes show up in four of them). Do each removal once and check it off when a later procedure asks again.

**Have open:** Ford workshop manual, Section 303-12: Intake Air System Components, 3.5L GTDI; Section 303-01A: Intake Manifold – Removal, Valve Cover LH – Removal, Valve Cover RH – Removal; Section 303-04A: Fuel Injection Pump – Removal, Fuel Injection Pump Roller Tappet Inspection; Section 303-07A: Ignition Coil-On-Plug.

**Tools / parts / time limits:**
- Phone camera: photograph hose and harness routing before you move anything.
- Compressed air, clean caps and plugs, trim-pin tools, flush cutters, 8/10/15 mm sockets, deep 10 mm.
- Discard: intake manifold gaskets, both valve cover gaskets, the 2 fuel pump bolts, the pump O-ring. Inspect coil seals, VCT solenoid seals and spark plug tube seals; replace any that are damaged.
- Battery stays disconnected. Fuel system stays open and capped. Keep every engine opening covered.

## Steps

### Air pipes and intake manifold
1. **Remove the engine appearance cover.** It lifts off its grommets. Put the oil fill cap back on right away so nothing falls in.
2. **Remove the air cleaner outlet pipe and the RH air cleaner-to-turbo pipe.** Back off the worm clamps; don't take the screws all the way out.
   Bag B03-01: 5 clamps (8 mm, stay on pipes), back in Stage 14, 5 Nm (44 lb-in).
3. **Disconnect the charge air cooler (CAC)-to-throttle body pipe.** Loosen the band clamp at the throttle body, twist the rubber end to break it loose, pull it off. (Ford figure N0128843 in "Intake Manifold – Removal".)
   Bag B03-02: 1 clamp (8 mm, stays on pipe), back in Stage 14, 5 Nm (44 lb-in).
4. **Unplug the throttle body.** Press the tab and pull the connector straight off. Pop the harness push pin below it out with a trim tool. (Ford figure N0128846.)
5. **Remove the PCV tube** (quick connects, fingers only).
   Bag B03-03: PCV tube, back in Stage 14, push-on.
6. **Disconnect the EVAP hose from the manifold.** Blow the coupling clean. Two side buttons: squeeze and pull. Lock tab on top: release it, then pull. No tools; they deform the clip.
7. **Unplug the MAP sensor and the purge valve,** then remove the purge valve bracket bolt and lay the valve aside with hoses on. (Ford figure N0128845.)
   Bag B03-04: 1 × 8 mm, back in Stage 14, 10 Nm (89 lb-in).
8. **Remove the 3 bolts and the appearance cover bracket.**
   Bag B03-05: 3 × 10 mm, back in Stage 14, 10 Nm (89 lb-in).
9. **Lift the T-shaped harness clip** straight up out of its post on the manifold. Grip the clip, not the wires. (Ford figure N0148681.)
10. **Pop the push pin on the turbo vacuum regulator** with a trim tool and lay the regulator aside, hoses attached. (Ford figure N0128847.)
    Bag B03-06: 1 push pin, back in Stage 14.
11. **Remove the intake manifold.** Loosen all 8 bolts (they're captive in the manifold) and lift it off. Throw away the gaskets. Stuff clean rags in the intake ports, then clean and inspect both sealing faces.
    Bag B03-07: 6 × 8 mm short, back in Stage 14, 10 Nm + 45° in Ford's sequence.
    Bag B03-08: 2 × 8 mm long, back in Stage 14, 10 Nm + 45° in Ford's sequence.

### LH pipes and fuel pump
12. **Remove the LH air cleaner-to-turbo pipe and the LH turbo-to-CAC tube.** The air cleaner outlet pipe is already off.
    Bag B03-09: 6 clamps across LH and RH turbo-to-CAC tubes (8 mm, stay on tubes), back in Stage 14, 5 Nm (44 lb-in).
13. **Remove the fuel pump noise shield.** Spread its openings a little as you lift; it tears easily.
    Bag B03-10: noise shield, back in Stage 14.
14. **Unplug the fuel pump.** Release any colored lock, press the tab, pull straight off. Don't rock it; the socket is part of the pump.
15. **Disconnect the low-pressure fuel jumper at the pump.** Blow it clean. The coupling has a lock tab on top: press it down with your thumb and pull straight off, no tools. (Ford figure N0161331 in "Fuel Injection Pump – Removal"; match the coupling type on your truck to Ford's "Quick Connect Coupling" procedure before you press anything.) Cap both ends. Fuel pressure was already relieved in Stage 2.
16. **Check the flare nut.** It was opened in Stage 2 and the bracket nut is already gone. Confirm the tube end is capped; don't touch it again. Opening only the pump end allows the tube to be reused.
17. **Remove the pump.** Loosen the 2 bolts one full turn at a time, alternating, so the cam lobe pushes the pump out evenly. Lift the pump off. Throw away both bolts and the O-ring.
    Bag B03-11: 2 × 10 mm, NEW at Stage 14, run down evenly one turn at a time until seated, then 20 Nm + 45°.
18. **Pull the roller tappet out of its bore** and inspect it. Look for flat spots or scoring on the roller. If you find any, inspect the pump and the drive lobe on the cam too, and replace what's damaged. Bag it clean.
    Bag B03-12: tappet, back in Stage 14. Oil the bore, lobe and tappet before it goes back.

### Coils
19. **Cut the tie strap** holding the turbo regulator hoses at the valve cover.
20. **Disconnect the 2 turbo regulator hoses.** Photograph first. Twist each hose to break it loose and pull straight off the nipple; don't bend it sideways.
21. **Remove the crankcase vent tube (2 quick connects).** Trace both ends first; don't assume it's the PCV tube you already pulled. If it's already off, note that and move on.
    Bag B03-13: vent tube, back in Stage 14.
22. **Blow out all 6 coil wells** with compressed air before anything comes out.
23. **Unplug the 6 coils.** Slide each connector's lock back toward the wires until it stops, press the tab, pull straight off. If it won't release, the lock isn't all the way back.
24. **Remove the 6 coil bolts and coils.** A slight twist breaks the boot seal.
    Bag B03-14: 6 × 8 mm, back in Stage 14, 7 Nm (62 lb-in).
25. **Inspect the coil seals** for rips, nicks or tears. Discard damaged ones and note how many new seals you need.

### LH valve cover
26. **Pull the dipstick.**
    Bag B03-15: dipstick, back in Stage 13.
27. **Cut the tie strap on the LH turbo wastegate hose** (flush cutters, blade away from the hose; pry the anchor out if it stays). (Ford figure N0146623 in "Valve Cover LH – Removal".)
28. **Disconnect the wastegate hose** at the round actuator by the turbo: twist and pull straight off, lay it aside. (Ford figure N0146624.)
29. **Remove the 2 nuts and the LH CAC tube bracket.**
    Bag B03-16: 1 × 10 mm small (M6), back in Stage 13, 6 Nm (53 lb-in).
    Bag B03-17: 1 × 15 mm large (M10), back in Stage 13, 48 Nm (35 lb-ft).
30. **Unplug the 2 LH VCT solenoids** at the front of the cover. Press each tab and pull straight up. The connector head turns slightly in its housing (about 12°); that's normal. Cut the tie strap below them. (Ford figure N0146628.)
31. **Release every harness clip from the LH cover and studs.** Trim tool under the clip base, lever straight up. Never pull the harness.
32. **Remove the LH valve cover.** Loosen all 11 bolts (they stay in the cover). Lift straight up. If it hangs on the VCT solenoids, wiggle it gently until the seals let go; don't pry against the solenoids. Throw away the gasket. Inspect the solenoids and replace any that are damaged.
    Bag B03-18: 11 bolt-and-sleeve (10 mm deep), back in Stage 13, 10 Nm (89 lb-in) in Ford's sequence.
33. **Inspect the 2 VCT solenoid seals and the spark plug tube seals in the cover.** Remove only damaged ones, using Ford's seal tools named in the procedure.
34. **Clean the sealing faces** of the cover, the head and the top of the front cover with metal surface prep.

### RH valve cover
35. **Remove the RH turbo-to-CAC tube.** The RH air cleaner pipe is already off and the coils are already out.
36. **Confirm the PCV tube is off.** It's the same tube you removed in step 5 (same Ford figure N0146625). Don't look for a second one.
37. **Remove the 2 nuts and the RH CAC tube bracket,** then free the coolant hose from the cover stud. Grip the clip, never the hose. (Ford figure N0128663 in "Valve Cover RH – Removal".)
    Bag B03-19: 2 × 10 mm, back in Stage 13, 6 Nm (53 lb-in).
38. **Cut the tie strap and disconnect the RH wastegate hose;** lay it aside. (Ford figure N0147481.)
39. **Cut the strap on the cover stud and pry out the push-in wire retainer** behind the big hose. (Ford figure N0147480.)
40. **Unplug the PCM connector.** Release the small lock where the lever latches, then swing the lever through its full travel; that pushes the connector out. If the lever won't move, the lock isn't released. Separate the inline connector below it and pry off the 2 wire retainers. Move the harness aside. (Ford figure N0129122.)
41. **Unplug the 2 RH VCT solenoids** and cut the strap holding the wastegate hose and wiring. (Ford figure N0146629.)
42. **Unplug the 2 cam position (CMP) sensors** at the front of the RH cover. (Ford figure N0146626.)
43. **Remove the 2 bolts and the 2 CMP sensors.**
    Bag B03-20: 2 × 8 mm, back in Stage 13, 7 Nm (62 lb-in).
44. **Release every harness clip from the RH cover and studs.**
45. **Remove the RH valve cover.** Loosen all 12 stud bolts (they stay in the cover), lift straight up, wiggle free of the VCT solenoids if it sticks. Throw away the gasket. Inspect and replace damaged solenoids. (Ford figure N0147482 shows the 12 locations.)
    Bag B03-21: 12 stud bolts (10 mm deep), back in Stage 13, 10 Nm (89 lb-in) in Ford's sequence.
46. **Inspect the RH VCT solenoid seals and spark plug tube seals;** remove only damaged ones.
47. **Clean the sealing faces** of the cover, head and front cover with metal surface prep.

## Done when
- Intake manifold, fuel pump, tappet, 6 coils and both valve covers are off.
- Every fastener is bagged and every hose and harness photographed.
- Roller tappet inspected; damaged coil, VCT and tube seals listed for replacement.
- Intake ports, fuel openings and engine openings covered.

## Stop if
- A fuel coupling doesn't match what you expected and you're unsure how it releases.
- A VCT solenoid connector is stuck or broken.
- You find debris inside the engine, contamination, or a scored or flat-spotted tappet.
