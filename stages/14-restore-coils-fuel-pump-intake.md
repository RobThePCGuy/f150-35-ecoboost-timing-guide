# Stage 14: Restore coils, fuel pump, intake and air pipes

This stage closes up the top of the engine: all six coils, the high-pressure fuel pump and its fittings, the intake manifold with new gaskets, and every pipe, hose, clamp and connector up top. The battery and the fuel pump control module (FPCM) stay disconnected until Stage 15. Keep the Ford procedures open alongside this page for the diagrams and exact wording.

**Have open:** Ford workshop manual: Ignition Coil-On-Plug (Removal and Installation); Fuel Injection Pump (Removal and Installation); Section 303-01A: Intake Manifold (Installation); Section 303-12: Intake Air System Components – Exploded View, 3.5L; Air Cleaner Outlet Pipe; Section 310-00: Quick Connect Coupling.

**Tools / parts / time limits:**
- Breaker bar on the crank pulley bolt; flashlight
- 8 mm, 10 mm and T30 sockets; inch-pound and foot-pound torque wrenches; angle gauge
- 17 mm crowfoot flare-nut wrench, mounted at 90° to the torque wrench handle
- Clean engine oil (5W-30)
- **New:** 2 fuel pump bolts, pump O-ring, fuel tube bracket nut, intake manifold gaskets. Coil boot seals only if damaged. Mounting plate seal and O-rings if they look worn.
- Dielectric grease, zip ties, 8 mm nut driver for the clamps
- **Crank turns clockwise only.** Pump cam lobe at bottom dead center (BDC) before the pump goes in.

## Steps

### Coils

1. **Coils in.** Put a small dab of dielectric grease inside each coil boot, press each coil down onto its plug until it seats, and torque the bolts to **7 Nm (62 lb-in)**. Replace any coil seal that's torn. Do all six. `From bag B03-14`
2. **Coil plugs.** Push each coil plug straight on until it clicks, then tug. Don't press the release tab while seating. A plug that won't click usually has a bent pin or debris in it; look before forcing.
3. **Crankcase vent tube (LH).** Clean both ends, push each coupling straight onto its fitting until it clicks, and pull back hard to prove it's locked. Close any secondary latch. No tools on the clips. `From bag B03-13`
4. **Vacuum hoses.** Put each small hose back on the nipple it came off; swapping them upsets boost control. Use your removal photos or tags, or trace each hose end to end. Push each one on until it bottoms and tug.
5. **Regulator hose tie.** New zip tie through the hose retainer at the valve cover, around the regulator hose. Snug, not pinched, and trim it flush so it can't rub the coil plugs.

### Fuel pump

6. **Take the plate off the pump bore.** Remove the mounting plate bolt and lift off the pump mounting plate so you can see the bore. `Bag B14-01: 1 × T30 bolt and plate, back in step 12, 10 Nm`
7. **Put the cam at BDC.** Breaker bar on the crank pulley bolt, turn **clockwise only** (standing in front of the truck). Shine a light down the pump bore and watch the lobe. Stop when it's at its lowest point with the round base of the cam facing up (Ford figure E182512 in "Fuel Injection Pump – Installation"). Overshot it? Keep going clockwise to the next low point. Never back up.
8. **Tappet.** Oil the pedestal bore, the cam lobe and the roller tappet with clean engine oil, then drop the tappet in. `From bag B03-12`
9. **Seals.** Inspect the mounting plate seal and its 2 O-rings; replace any that are nicked or flattened. Oil them all. Oil the new pump O-ring and fit it on the pump.
10. **Assemble.** Wipe both mating faces clean. Fit the mounting plate onto the pump and start the 2 new pump bolts through it.
11. **Seat the pump evenly.** Set the pump and plate on the pedestal and start the 2 new bolts and the plate bolt loosely. Bring the pump bolts down one full turn each, alternating, until the pump is seated. Then torque each to **20 Nm (177 lb-in)**, then turn each **an additional 45°** (Ford figure E182903 in "Fuel Injection Pump – Installation"). `From bag B03-11 (new bolts)`
12. **Plate bolt.** Torque the mounting plate bolt to **10 Nm (89 lb-in)**. `From bag B14-01`
13. **Fuel tube, finger-tight first.** Oil the threads of the high-pressure tube flare nut. Start the flare nut on the pump and the **new** bracket nut on the bracket, both finger-tight only (Ford figure E184017 in "Fuel Injection Pump – Installation"). `From bags B02-02 and B02-01 (new nut)`
14. **Torque the flare nut.** Put the 17 mm crowfoot on the torque wrench at 90° to the handle. At 90° the wrench reads true, so there's nothing to calculate. Tighten to **15 Nm (133 lb-in)**. Then set the wrench to **50 Nm (37 lb-ft)** and turn the nut another **30°** (one-twelfth of a turn) with the angle gauge. Stop at 30° or when the wrench clicks at 50 Nm, **whichever comes first**.
15. **Then the bracket nut.** Only after the flare nut is done, torque the bracket nut to **8 Nm (71 lb-in)**.
16. **Low-pressure coupling.** Push the jumper tube coupling straight onto the pump inlet until it clicks, pull back to prove it's locked, then press the raised secondary latch down flush (Ford figure N0161331). If the latch won't go flush, the coupling isn't fully on. No tools on the clip.
17. **Pump plug.** Push the plug onto the pump solenoid until it clicks and tug it. Keep the side latch clear of the shield (Ford figure E182892).
18. **Noise shield.** Spread the shield's openings gently and fit it over the pump without tearing it. `From bag B03-10`

### Intake manifold

19. **Check for debris.** If this engine ever had valve or piston damage, look inside the manifold for metal. Any metal means a new manifold.
20. **Manifold down.** Fit new gaskets, set the manifold, start all 8 bolts by hand (the 2 long ones go in their own holes), then torque to **10 Nm (89 lb-in)** in Ford's sequence and turn each **an additional 45°** in the same order (Ford figure N0124240 in "Intake Manifold – Installation"). `From bags B03-07 and B03-08`
21. **Vacuum regulator.** Line the regulator's hole up and push the pin through until the head sits flat; if it has a center plunger, pull it up first and press it flush after. Use a new pin if the old one's legs broke (Ford figure N0128847). `From bag B03-06`
22. **Harness clip.** Push the T-shaped harness clip's stem into its boss on the manifold until it snaps (Ford figure N0148681).
23. **Appearance cover bracket.** Install the bracket and 3 bolts at **10 Nm (89 lb-in)**. `From bag B03-05`
24. **Purge valve and MAP.** Set the EVAP purge valve back and torque its bolt to **10 Nm (89 lb-in)**. Plug in the MAP sensor and the purge valve. `From bag B03-04`
25. **EVAP hose.** Blow off the end and push the coupling onto the manifold fitting until it clicks; pull back hard and close any latch (Ford figure N0146627).
26. **PCV tube.** Click both quick-connect ends on and pull-test them. `From bag B03-03`
27. **Throttle body plug.** Push it on until it clicks, then push the harness pin retainer into its hole on the throttle body (Ford figure N0128846).
28. **CAC-to-throttle-body pipe.** Push the pipe fully onto the throttle body neck with its locating tab in the notch. Sit the clamp just behind the raised bead, not on it, and tighten to **5 Nm (44 lb-in)** (Ford figure N0128843). Over-tightening cuts the coupler. `From bag B03-02`

### Air pipes and final audit

29. **Air cleaner pipes.** Install the RH air-cleaner-to-turbo pipe and the air cleaner outlet pipe, and on the LH side the air-cleaner-to-turbo pipe, matching Ford's 3.5L intake air exploded view. Clamps at **5 Nm (44 lb-in)**. `From bag B03-01`
30. **Turbo-to-CAC tubes.** Reconnect both turbo-to-CAC tubes, clamps at **5 Nm (44 lb-in)**. `From bag B03-09`
31. **Walk the whole top end.** Check every pipe and clamp, the PCV and crankcase vent, EVAP couplings, wastegate and regulator hoses, grounds and harness retainers against the Ford figures and your removal photos.
32. **Clear the deck.** Remove every cap, plug, rag and tool from the engine bay. Install the engine appearance cover and the oil filler cap.

## Done when
- All six coils at 7 Nm, plugs clicked, vent tube and vacuum hoses back where they came from
- Pump in on new bolts (20 Nm + 45°), plate bolt 10 Nm, flare nut done (15 Nm then 30° or 50 Nm), new bracket nut 8 Nm after it
- Low-pressure coupling latched flush, pump plug clicked, shield on
- Manifold on new gaskets, 10 Nm + 45° in sequence
- Every pipe, clamp, connector and the appearance cover restored; no caps or rags left anywhere
- Battery negative and FPCM still disconnected

## Stop if
- The cam lobe isn't at BDC, or the crank fights you while turning clockwise
- The fuel tube or flare seat is damaged, or the flare nut won't start by hand
- A quick-connect latch won't close or a connector won't latch
- You find metal in the intake manifold
