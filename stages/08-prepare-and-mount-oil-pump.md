# Stage 8: Prepare and mount the oil pump

You read the new pump's instructions, prime it, bolt it to the block, refit the cleaned pickup with a new O-ring, and slide the crank sprocket back on. The pump is a Melling M390HV (or the OE Ford pump). Whatever its maker's sheet says about mounting, seals or priming comes first; the Ford values below apply once the sheet agrees with them.

**Have open:**
- Ford workshop manual, Section 303-01A: Oil Pump – Installation (steps 1–4)
- The instruction sheet that came in the pump box

**Tools / parts / time limits:**
- Melling M390HV or OE pump, with its instruction sheet
- The original pickup, cleaned and passed in Stage 6 (or a Melling 397S)
- New pickup O-ring
- Clean engine oil, a tablespoon measure, 8 mm socket, small torque wrench that reads 10 Nm accurately

## Steps

1. **Read the pump maker's instruction sheet first.** Check it against the Ford pump you removed: mounting holes, pickup flange, seals and priming. Follow the sheet wherever it differs from the steps below. Leave the relief spring as the pump came. The M390HV ships with a 100 psi bypass spring and an optional 85 psi spring; those numbers are spring ratings, not a gauge pressure to aim for, and you don't swap or touch the relief plug in this job.
2. **Prime the pump.** Set it on the bench, intake side up. Pour about 2 tablespoons of clean engine oil into the pickup inlet port. Turn the inner gear by hand through its center bore several full turns, until the oil smears around and you feel it pull. Note the 2 tablespoons for your oil fill count later. Install it right away so the oil stays in.
3. **Mount the pump** with its 3 bolts. Run them in by hand, then torque to 10 Nm (89 lb-in). (Ford figure N0148009 in "Oil Pump – Installation")
   From bag B06-22
4. **Fit the pickup.** Put a new O-ring on the pickup tube, push it squarely into the pump, and install the 3 bolts. Torque to 10 Nm (89 lb-in). (Ford figure N0120779 in "Oil Pump – Installation")
   From bag B06-21
5. **Slide the crank sprocket on with its timing dot facing out**, toward you. (Ford figure N0148008 in "Oil Pump – Installation")

## Done when
- Pump instruction sheet read and any difference from the Ford pump settled
- Relief spring left as delivered
- Pump primed, mounted, 3 bolts at 10 Nm
- Pickup on with a new O-ring, 3 bolts at 10 Nm
- Crank sprocket on, dot out
- Priming oil noted for the fill count

## Stop if
- The instruction sheet is missing
- The pump's mounting, pickup flange or seals don't match the block or the pickup
- The pump doesn't turn freely by hand after priming
- You're tempted to change the relief spring
