# F-150 3.5L EcoBoost timing chain, phaser and oil pump guide

A step-by-step guide to replacing the timing chains, guides, tensioners, VCT phasers and solenoids, and the oil pump on a **2011–2014 Ford F-150 3.5L EcoBoost**. It was written from a completed job on a 2013 4WD. The truck runs strong afterward.

It's built for doing the job at home: what to do at every step, how each clip and connector actually comes apart, every bolt bagged and tracked from the moment it comes out until it goes back in, and the misfire relearn at the end.

## Before you start

- **Get Ford's workshop manual.** This guide follows Ford's procedures and points to Ford's figures by ID (for example "Ford figure N0118126"), but doesn't reproduce them. A short-term subscription at the Motorcraft Service / Ford PTS site gives you every procedure and diagram. Have the matching procedure open as you work.
- **Read [PARTS.md](PARTS.md)** and order everything first.
- **Set up the bolt system in [BOLTS.md](BOLTS.md)**: two tables, numbered spots, labeled bags. Reassembly is where this job goes wrong; the bags are how it doesn't.
- **The misfire relearn is required after this job** ([RELEARN.md](RELEARN.md)). Confirm before teardown that FORScan lists it for your truck; it takes five minutes.

## Notes

- Stages 6 and 9 follow Ford's 4X4 oil pan procedure. On a 2WD truck, use Ford's 4X2 oil pan procedure for the axle and crossmember steps.
- Socket sizes marked "unconfirmed" in the bolt ledger weren't confirmed for this engine. Corrections welcome as issues or pull requests.
- You're working on fuel, cooling and timing systems. Follow Ford's warnings in the workshop manual, support the truck on stands, and relieve fuel pressure before opening any fuel line.
